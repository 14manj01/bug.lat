PrimedSherlock: a tool for rapid design of highly specific CRISPR-Cas12 crRNAs

Mann JG, Pitts RJ

BMC Bioinformatics 23:428

CRISPR-Cas assays bring lab-grade specificity to the field, but designing their guide RNAs and isothermal primers takes intensive manual curation. PrimedSherlock automates it, selecting highly specific crRNAs from PrimedRPA primer sets. Guides designed against West Nile, Zika, and all four dengue serotypes detected their targets with minimal off-target noise.

Aedes japonicus japonicus in Nebraska and Texas

Sames WJ, Hamik J, Mann JG, Bast JD, Pitts RJ

Journal of the American Mosquito Control Association 38(2):92–95

Documents the continued westward spread of the Asian rock pool mosquito, recording it in eight Nebraska counties and in Bowie County, Texas: the species’ first record in the state, found while surveying surplus military equipment at a Texas Army depot.

Feeding habits of vector mosquitoes in Harris County, TX, 2018

Mann JG, Washington M, Guynup T, Tarrand C, Dewey EM, Fredregill C, Duguma D, Pitts RJ

Journal of Medical Entomology 57(6):1920–1929

Using COI DNA barcoding to identify bloodmeal hosts across urban and semi-urban Houston, we found heavy use of birds, even by the human-biting Aedes aegypti, and evidence of multiple bloodmeals in about half of specimens. Frequent feeding on backyard chickens may dampen West Nile virus transmission locally.
